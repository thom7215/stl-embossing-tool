Letter height for text. Longest side of the artwork for an SVG. The outline thickness stays in millimetres.

Letters stand proud of the face by this thickness. Flush letters end level with the face, and the thickness is how far they extend into the model.

The next SVG lands on the face in the middle of the view, or flat on the print bed. It imports as the artwork only. Set Outline above 0 while it is selected to add one.
